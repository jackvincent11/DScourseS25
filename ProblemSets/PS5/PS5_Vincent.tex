\section{Data Sets}
This data interests me because it shows the best performances in history in one of the showcase events of track and field, the 1500-meter race. You can use it to compare and contrast when the fastest athletes have existed, if multiple performances were ran in a single race or similar time period, etc. 

The second data set comes from the FRED website, showing S\&P 500 index levels beginning in 2020. This data is useful and interesting to me because it could be used to identify trends or cyclical patterns in the stock index. To load this data set, I used the "fredr" package.
